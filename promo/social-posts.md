## LinkedIn

**Image:** promo-features-1200x628.png

**Post:**

Excited to share something I've been building: DSCVR.

It's a research desk designed for how people actually work with papers and sources — not how tools assume they do.

The core idea: one app where you save papers, PDFs, YouTube lectures, and notes; discover new research from arXiv and Semantic Scholar; and organize everything in a structure that mirrors how you think.

A few things I'm particularly proud of:

Parliament Fact-Check uses three AI perspectives (Advocate, Skeptic, Analyst) to evaluate claims. Instead of a single AI opinion, you get a verdict from a panel. Built on Anthropic's Claude with multi-model tool_use voting.

Pinda (coming soon) is an AI assistant that reads your saved papers and answers questions with citations pointing back to your own materials. Not a generic chatbot — it knows what you've read.

The technical stack: Flutter for cross-platform (mobile + PWA), Firebase for auth and storage, a dedicated Python Flask server for security and API proxying, and Claude for the AI features.

Works on any device. Installs as a PWA. Offline-ready with bundled fonts. Figma-style link sharing for collaboration.

Now in beta — try the demo, no sign-up required.
