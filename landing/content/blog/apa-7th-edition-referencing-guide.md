APA style, from the American Psychological Association, is used across psychology, education, nursing, social sciences and many other subjects. The 7th edition is current. Here are the formats you'll use most.

## In-text citations

APA uses author-date citations in the text.

- **One author:** (Nguyen, 2021) or Nguyen (2021) found...
- **Two authors:** (Nguyen & Patel, 2021). In narrative form, use "and": Nguyen and Patel (2021).
- **Three or more authors:** (Nguyen et al., 2021) from the first citation onward.
- **Direct quotes** need a page or paragraph number: (Nguyen, 2021, p. 14).
- **Group authors:** (World Health Organization, 2022).

## Journal article

> Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of the article in sentence case. *Journal Name, volume*(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx

> Rivera, M., & Chen, L. (2020). Sleep and memory consolidation in adolescents. *Journal of Adolescent Health, 67*(3), 312–318. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.example.2020.01.001

The authors, article and DOI are made up to show the format. Include the DOI as a full link whenever there is one.

## Book

> Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of the book in sentence case* (edition). Publisher.

> Kumar, S. (2019). *Research methods for nursing* (3rd ed.). Example Press.

(A made-up example.)

## Chapter in an edited book

> Author, A. A. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor (Ed.), *Title of book* (pp. pages). Publisher.

## Webpage

> Author or Organization. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL

If the author and the site name are the same, leave out the site name.

## Reference list rules

- Title the page **References**, centred and bold.
- List entries alphabetically by the first author's surname.
- Use a hanging indent.
- Up to 20 authors are listed in full in the reference list.
- Article titles use sentence case; journal names use title case and italics, as does the volume number.

## Common mistakes

- Using "and" instead of "&" inside parentheses.
- Writing out "et al." for two-author works.
- Leaving out the DOI.
- Capitalising every word in an article title.
