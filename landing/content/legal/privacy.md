Effective 11 November 2026.

This policy explains what DSCVR collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. DSCVR ("we", "us") is operated by Anthony Ude. If anything here is unclear, write to [mail@askpinda.com](mailto:mail@askpinda.com).

## The short version

- We collect what we need to run your account and your library, and nothing for advertising.
- Your library, notes, Pinda conversations and reports are private to you unless you share them.
- We send your questions and the material you choose to our AI provider so Pinda can answer. They are not used to train models.
- We don't sell your data. There are no ads and no third-party trackers on the app or this site.
- You can delete your account, and everything in it, from Settings at any time.

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your name, email address and password (stored by our sign-in provider, never by us in plain text), or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google. Your edition (Students, Researchers or Enterprise), your field, and the subjects you follow.

**Your library.** Categories, topics, papers, links, videos, notes and PDFs you add; your margin notes, ratings and reading progress; Pinda conversations, study sets (flashcards, practice questions, case briefs, evidence summaries), reports, focus sessions and their recaps.

**Group sessions.** If you join a live session, your display name, your messages and the papers you share are visible to everyone in that session.

**Community ratings.** When you rate a published paper, we store your rating against your account and show other readers only the average and the number of ratings, never who rated.

**Devices.** For each signed-in device we store a label (for example "Chrome on Mac"), the platform and when it was last active, so you can see your devices and sign them out.

**Usage and billing.** How much of your Pinda allowance you have used, which features used it, and token counts. If you buy a plan, your plan, its status, renewal date and the customer and subscription references from our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

**Technical logs.** Our server keeps short-lived logs of requests (time, path, status and errors) to keep the service running and secure.

## What we don't collect

We don't run advertising, analytics or tracking scripts in the app or on this site. We don't buy data about you, and we don't build profiles for anyone else.

## How we use it

- To provide DSCVR: store your library, sync it across devices, run search, generate Pinda answers, study sets, reports and recaps.
- To keep your account secure, prevent abuse and enforce fair-use limits.
- To bill paid plans and Enterprise organisations.
- To send the emails you ask for, such as password resets and, if you turn them on, alert digests.
- To answer you when you contact support.

## Who we share it with

We use the following service providers to run DSCVR. Each receives only what it needs.

| Provider | What for | What they receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Firebase | Sign-in, database, file storage | Your account and library data |
| Railway | Hosting our server | Requests to our server and logs |
| Anthropic | The AI model behind Pinda | Your question and the material you chose for that answer |
| Paystack | Card payments | Your email and payment details you enter on their page |
| Resend | Alert digest emails (only if you turn them on) | Your email address and the alert titles |

**Scholarly sources.** When you search, our server sends your search words, but not your identity, to the sources Discover covers, such as arXiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, Crossref, DOAJ, PLOS, Zenodo, Unpaywall and the UK National Archives' Find Case Law. Topic summaries are fetched from Wikipedia by your browser.

**Voice.** Dictation and read-aloud use the speech service built into your browser or device. Depending on your browser, that service may process audio on its provider's servers under the provider's own policy. DSCVR does not record or store your audio.

**Sharing you choose.** If you create a share link, anyone who opens it while signed in can see the titles, summaries, links and notes in that collection. Files you uploaded, margin notes, ratings and Pinda chats are never included.

**Enterprise.** If you join an organisation, its admins can see your name, email, role, and your daily activity and usage for billing. They cannot read your library.

**Legal reasons.** We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the service.

We never sell your personal information.

## AI and your content

Pinda answers from the material you choose, which is sent to our AI provider with your question. Under our agreement with that provider, your content is not used to train their models. AI answers can be wrong; check important answers against the source.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete an item it is removed from your library. When you delete your account from Settings, your profile and library are deleted. Copies in backups and logs are overwritten within 30 days. Billing records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can view, edit, export (reports and citations) and delete your content in the app at any time, and delete your whole account from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, correct it, restrict or object to processing, or complain to your local data protection authority. Email [mail@askpinda.com](mailto:mail@askpinda.com) and we'll respond within 30 days.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your library is limited to your account by database rules, and our server checks your sign-in on every request. You can see and sign out your devices in Settings. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

## Children

DSCVR is not meant for children under 13. If you're under the age required to consent to data processing where you live, ask a parent or guardian before using it. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## International use

Our providers store and process data in several countries, including the United States and the European Union. We rely on their standard safeguards for these transfers.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
