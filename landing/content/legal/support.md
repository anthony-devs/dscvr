## Common questions

### I can't sign in

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page to get a reset email. If you signed up with Google, choose "Continue with Google" instead. Still stuck? Email us from the address on your account.

### Pinda says I've reached my limit

The free plan includes a daily Pinda allowance that resets every day. The meter in Settings, under Plan and usage, shows how much you've used. Student Plus, Research Plus and Max give Pinda more room.

### How do I cancel my plan?

Go to Plans in the app and choose Cancel. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you've paid for.

### A paper won't open in the reader

Some papers don't have a free PDF. Use "Find a free PDF" on the paper, or "Open original" to read it on the publisher's site. You can also upload a PDF you already have.

### How do I share a collection?

Open a category or topic and choose Share. Create a link that's view-only, or view-and-copy so people can save their own copy. Anyone with the link can open it after signing in. Files you uploaded and your private notes are never shared.

### How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your profile and your whole library.

### Is my data used to train AI?

No. Pinda sends your question and the material you chose to our AI provider to write the answer, and it isn't used to train their models. See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for details.

## Report a problem

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps. For security issues, put "Security" in the subject line.
