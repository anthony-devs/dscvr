AskPinda builds tools that make serious reading less painful. DSCVR is our first product.

## What we're doing

Most people who study or research spend more time hunting for sources, getting past paywalls and keeping notes straight than actually thinking. DSCVR puts the search, the reading and the writing in one place: it searches free scholarly sources, textbooks and archives together, opens the PDFs, and lets Pinda answer from what you saved, with the passage it used.

## How we work

- **Free first.** We search open sources and look for legal, free copies of papers. We do not scrape paywalls.
- **Your library is yours.** Private unless you share it, deletable at any time, never used to train models.
- **Answers you can check.** Pinda points to where in your material an answer came from.
- **Honest about limits.** If a source is down or a plan has a limit, we say so.
