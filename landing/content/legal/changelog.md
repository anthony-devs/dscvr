What's new in DSCVR, newest first.

## November 2026

- **Textbooks and course notes in Discover.** Search "databases" or "calculus" and get free textbooks and study guides from OpenStax, the Open Textbook Library and Wikibooks, not only research papers. There is a new **Textbooks & notes** option next to All sources.
- **Email you can trust.** Confirm-your-email, welcome and password-reset emails now come from AskPinda, and every update or digest has a one-click unsubscribe.
- **Terms and updates, your choice.** Sign-up asks you to accept the Terms and Privacy Policy, and lets you opt in to product updates.
- **History and archives.** Free scanned books and documents from the Internet Archive, the Library of Congress and Europeana, with filters for region, period and type.
- **Endless results.** Discover now loads more as you scroll.
- **Faster, steadier loading.** The app opens from a saved copy on slow connections, and big scanned PDFs open reliably.
- **A new home.** DSCVR now runs on Google Cloud, with the API at api.askpinda.com.
